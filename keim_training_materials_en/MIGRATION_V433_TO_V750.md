# Migration v4.3.3 → v7.5 Enterprise

This English training edition keeps the complete original beginner course and adds v7.5 Enterprise chapters. The old course remains the foundation for beginners.

## Most important rule

Do not start by explaining Enterprise architecture. Teach the basics first, then show artefact chains.

## Recommended order

1. Chapters 1–8: programming foundations
2. Chapters 9–15: structure, modules, HTTP and data
3. Chapters 16–20: debugging, projects and reference
4. Chapters 21–24: Keim v7.5 architecture and KBC-STABLE-1
5. Chapters 25–29: Result/Match, security, tests and hardening
6. Chapters 30–33/35: Training Impossible, labs, packaging and final exam

## Preserved content

The original German edition remains in the repository. Legacy examples remain available in `examples/legacy/`.
